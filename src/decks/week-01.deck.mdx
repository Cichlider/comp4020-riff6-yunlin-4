# Via negativa

SLOP3268 --- Week 1

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## "Anything you can say about it is already too small."

--- the apophatic move, fifteen centuries before it needed a name

---

## The negative way

Pseudo-Dionysius, describing God:

- not good
- not wise
- not existent

Not because these are false --- because every word borrowed from
creation is smaller than what it's describing.

---

## The secular version

Nassim Taleb, "subtractive knowledge":

- we're often more certain about what fails than what works
- expertise is accumulated omission, more than accumulated fact
- this course tests that claim against a harder case than his

---

{/* _class: centered */}

## The question, every week

What did leaving this out cost?

What did it buy?

---

## Twelve weeks, one move

theology · painting · music · fiction · film · architecture · design ·
statistics · mathematics · politics --- then a week that turns the
question on the course itself

1. **Commonplace Book of Cuts** --- 20%, week 5

Each one bigger, each one asking you to trust the cut more.
