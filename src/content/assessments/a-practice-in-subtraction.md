## The brief

Make one finished piece --- writing, image, sound, video, code, a
proof, a design, anything with a defined medium --- built around a single
omission you chose on purpose. Submit the piece and a written defence
of at least 300 words: what you left out, why it was harder to cut than
to keep, and what the piece would have lost by keeping it.

## Submission format

The piece itself, in whatever format its medium requires, plus the
defence as a separate Markdown or PDF file. If the piece is time-based
(audio, video, animation), keep it under three minutes: the mark is for
the cut, not the runtime.

## What this is testing

Whether the semester's reading translates into your own practice, not
just your ability to describe someone else's cut after the fact. The
defence is marked as strictly as the piece --- a strong piece with a weak
defence, or a weak piece with a defence that argues its way around the
work rather than through it, both mark down.
